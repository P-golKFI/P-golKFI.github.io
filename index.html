Pre-launch · field-tested with workers

I taught myself Flutter and built it. Workers and employers in Siliguri, Asansol and Durgapur find, verify and rate each other. The app never touches the money.

Chapter 5 · What the data said

The fraud detector caught the wrong people.

~8×

The best honest workers got flagged about eight times as often as everyone else. A great worker looks exactly like a boosted one.

Other findings: account-age defences stop working once fake accounts are about nine months old, and hiding ratings from the person rated takes away the reason to retaliate.

The paper · CREST Gold submission

Reputation Without Retaliation: Pricing Manipulation and Designing Out Retaliation in Bidirectional Rating Systems for Indian Gig Platforms
